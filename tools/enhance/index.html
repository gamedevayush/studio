How do I get a Gemini API key?
  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and press Create API key. Gemini's image models are paid per image (no free tier), so the key's project needs billing turned on. Google may ask for a small prepay the first time.
  2. Have Google AI Pro? It includes $10 of Google Cloud credit every month. Apply it to your billing account at developers.google.com/program/my-benefits and AI Studio spends that credit first.
  3. Paste the key (AIza…) above.

Your key is kept only in this browser and is sent only to Google. It is never stored on this site.
